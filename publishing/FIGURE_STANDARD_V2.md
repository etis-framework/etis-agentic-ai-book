# ETIS book figure standard — V2 (review candidate)

- Figure must be legible on a **7 × 10 inch printed page** at its intended width; not merely at 100% SVG zoom.
- Prefer 20–26 px labels in a 960-unit SVG, sparse annotation, no separate explanatory legend box.
- Diagram family must match the concept: comparison, architecture, sequence, or conceptual spectrum.
- Editable **SVG is the master** for these seven illustrations; PNG is a generated compatibility image. The earlier 22 DOT masters remain DOT → SVG → PNG.
- Verify embedded image count and inspect actual Word page renders; a successful image render is not publication approval.
- Keep captions in the manuscript, not repeated as a giant legend under the image.
