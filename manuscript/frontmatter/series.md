# Series page

The ETIS Professional Computing Series develops the professional judgment required to understand, verify, build, operate, and steward trustworthy systems in the AI era.

*Engineering Agentic AI Systems* applies that discipline to the design and operation of AI agents: bounding their authority, independently controlling consequential actions, verifying claims against evidence, and engineering reliable recovery and human accountability. Its six progressive Python builds provide executable learning evidence, not production certification.
