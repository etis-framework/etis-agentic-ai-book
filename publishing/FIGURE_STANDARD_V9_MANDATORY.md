# ETIS Figure Standard — mandatory Chapter 4 onward

Use print-first layouts at the book's final 5.75-inch figure width. Prefer 22–24 pt Arial source node labels for Graphviz diagrams; this is a starting point, **not** an effective-font-size guarantee. Avoid small edge labels: use shorter node text and explain detailed semantics in the caption. Maintain consistent navy outlines, pale-blue process boxes, amber decision/authority boxes, green verified outcomes, rounded rectangles, and clear arrowheads. No unreviewed diagonal or looping connector routing. A figure is accepted only after visual inspection at actual 7 × 10 print size, including caption and wrapped labels. Never use E1–E9 shorthand inside teaching figures unless a companion legend is essential and directly adjacent. Preserve DOT masters and rendered SVG/PNG. Record chapter-by-chapter signoff.
